Students

  1. Join the teacher's room with your name or initials. Capacity is selected before opening, from 20 to 40 seats.
  2. Start with $1,500 virtual chips. Choose a wager of $100 or more, in $50 steps up to your balance. Lock it before seeing the question.
  3. The teacher deals; a three-second cue is followed by a shared 30-second answer window. Your first accepted answer locks.
  4. Correct returns your stake and pays matching profit. Applied bonus +$50; Advanced bonus +$100. Wrong or timeout loses your stake.
  5. Below $100 means out. A willing student can rescue you between hands by giving half of current net winnings above their original $1,500. The gift must fund your next wager and preserve the donor's already locked wager. One rescue received by default.
  6. The hand limit ends the game. Fewer than two active students after the rescue window also ends play. Highest final balance wins; correct answers break a balance tie; an exact tie is shared.

If you can play but do not lock a wager, the deal automatically commits $100. Disconnection does not cancel your wager. Answers must reach the teacher before the deadline.

Teacher preparation

Teach the motor lesson, then open the teacher portal. Choose Live classroom, seat capacity, question sources, chapters, skill levels, and hand limit. Default capacity is 24. Default chapter rotation is two motor hands for each circuitry hand.

Share the Copy student link result. Wait for your intended roster to show Connected. Explain each revealed answer, allow rescue decisions, then select Next hand. Export CSV results before closing the host tab.
